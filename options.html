⚠️ Secondary Rate Limit
GitHub enforces a secondary rate limit (not shown in API) that limits:
  • No more than 100 concurrent requests
  • No more than 900 points per minute for REST API
  • No more than 90 seconds of CPU time per 60 seconds
If you get 403 errors even with remaining calls, you may have hit the secondary limit. Wait 1 minute and retry.
